# GatherLite 8.1.7

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added 817 unique locations: 471 herbs, 248 ore, and 98 containers. The largest gains are Silverleaf (+204), Copper Vein (+194), and Peacebloom (+183), including their cataloged starter variants.
- Added 54 Battered Chest, 20 Battered Footlocker, and 14 Waterlogged Footlocker locations among the container gains.
- The bundled database now has 29,208 unique locations. All 28,391 locations from 8.1.6 are preserved.

Both Forever gathering listings and all 152 relevant object pages were freshly validated. Five listed objects remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779) and Hyjal Silphium (654624) have no published map coordinates; Chunk of Amber (656332) has 21, Large Chunk of Amber (656333) has 44, and Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but still need resource classification. These five are not represented as new resources in this package. Seven known quest pickups are excluded.
