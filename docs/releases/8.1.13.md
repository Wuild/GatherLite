# GatherLite 8.1.13

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added **43 unique locations**: 19 herbs, 12 ore, and 12 containers. Battered Footlocker gained nine, Silverleaf seven, Peacebloom five, and Silver Vein five.
- The bundled database now has **29,952 unique locations**. All 29,909 locations from 8.1.12 are preserved.

Both Forever gathering listings and all 152 relevant object pages were freshly validated. Five listed objects remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779) and Hyjal Silphium (654624) have no published map coordinates; Chunk of Amber (656332) has 21, Large Chunk of Amber (656333) has 51, and Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but still need resource classification. These five are not represented as new resources in this package. Seven known quest pickups are excluded.
