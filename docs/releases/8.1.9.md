# GatherLite 8.1.9

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added 195 unique locations: 105 herbs, 54 ore, and 36 containers. Peacebloom gained 49, Silverleaf 40, and Copper Vein 35.
- The bundled database now has 29,574 unique locations. All 29,379 locations from 8.1.8 are preserved.

Both Forever gathering listings and all 152 relevant object pages were freshly validated. Five listed objects remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779) and Hyjal Silphium (654624) have no published map coordinates; Chunk of Amber (656332) has 21, Large Chunk of Amber (656333) has 46, and Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but still need resource classification. These five are not represented as new resources in this package. Seven known quest pickups are excluded.
