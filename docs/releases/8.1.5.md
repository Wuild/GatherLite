# GatherLite 8.1.5

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added 265 [Wilted Peacebloom](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=656160) locations and grouped the variant with Peacebloom.
- Added 274 [Stunted Silverleaf](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=656161) locations and grouped the variant with Silverleaf.
- These 539 unique positions span Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades, Dun Morogh, Elwynn Forest, and Teldrassil. The bundled database now has 28,113 locations; all 27,574 previously released locations are preserved.

The full Forever mining and herb listings and every known source page were refreshed and validated. Two newly listed objects remain outside the catalog: Hyjal Silphium (654624) has no published map coordinates, while Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but still needs gathering-catalog classification. Neither is represented as a new resource in this package.
