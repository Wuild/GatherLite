# GatherLite 8.1.6

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added 266 [Poor Copper Vein](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=562111) positions in Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades, Dun Morogh, and Elwynn Forest. This verified starter variant is grouped with Copper Vein in resource browsing and gathering tracking.
- Added 12 unique footlocker positions from refreshed object pages.
- The bundled database now has 28,391 unique positions. All 28,113 positions from 8.1.5 are preserved.

Both Forever gathering listings and all 152 relevant object pages were freshly validated. Five listed objects remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779) and Hyjal Silphium (654624) have no published map coordinates; Chunk of Amber (656332) has 19, Large Chunk of Amber (656333) has 31, and Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but need resource classification. These five are not represented as new resources in this package. Seven known quest pickups are excluded.
