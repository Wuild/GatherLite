# GatherLite 8.1.16

Database update for WoW Forever 1.60.1.

- Added **49 unique locations**: 21 herbs, 3 ore, and 25 containers.
- Herbs include 13 Peacebloom and 8 Silverleaf positions; ore adds 3 Copper Vein positions.
- Containers include 17 Battered Footlocker, 3 Waterlogged Footlocker, 1 Practice Lockbox, and 4 other chest positions.
- The database now contains **30,129 unique locations**, up from 30,080. All previous coordinates are preserved.

Completed the October 12 refresh on October 13 by resuming only missing pages. Both gathering listings and all 152 object pages are validated; the final discovery/import ran offline from the complete cache. This establishes coverage of Wowhead's published sources, not every gathering object in the game.

Five listed IDs remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779, 0 coordinates), Hyjal Silphium (654624, 0), Chunk of Amber (656332, 21), Large Chunk of Amber (656333, 43), and Tomb Weed (694732, 20). Their coordinates are excluded pending catalog verification. Seven known quest pickups are excluded from gathering discovery.
