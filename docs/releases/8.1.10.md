# GatherLite 8.1.10

A gathering database update for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- Added 104 unique locations: 38 herbs, 37 ore, and 29 containers. Peacebloom gained 15, Silverleaf 14, Copper Vein 16, and Battered Footlocker 19.
- The bundled database now has 29,678 unique locations. All 29,574 locations from 8.1.9 are preserved.

Both Forever gathering listings and all 152 relevant object pages were freshly validated. Five listed objects remain outside the catalog: Heavy Thorium Vein (566779) and Hyjal Silphium (654624) have no published map coordinates; Chunk of Amber (656332) has 21, Large Chunk of Amber (656333) has 47, and Tomb Weed (694732) has 20 published positions but still need resource classification. These five are not represented as new resources in this package. Seven known quest pickups are excluded.
