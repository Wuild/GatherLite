# GatherLite 8.2.2

- Completed routes save their ground geometry and restore after login or reload without recalculating terrain paths. Saved routes are validated against the addon version, client build, resource selection, zone scope and predefined-data setting.
- Legacy saves without geometry rebuild only the selected zone.
- Ground route calculations use up to 32 representative stops instead of 64, reducing terrain searches in dense gathering areas.
- Zone-only browsing and route collection use indexed locations instead of scanning unrelated zones.
- Fixed repeated map hover errors when the client does not provide the gamepad API.
- Added Heavy Thorium Vein, Chunk of Amber, Large Chunk of Amber, Hyjal Silphium and Tomb Weed to the resource catalog.
- Refreshed the Forever database to 30,544 locations, 85 more than 8.2.0, preserving every previously released coordinate and starter-variant ID.
- Newly gathered locations appear immediately in open maps and resource lists.
- Nearby minimap markers can be hidden or shown as circles; overlapping markers share a tooltip. Automatic gathering tracking handles client entries without spell IDs.
- Online node database updates now always use graphical Chromium, started minimized.
